# Code of Conduct

This project is a learning space. Many people here are making their first contribution, and everyone deserves to feel welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners
- Give feedback on code, never on the person
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge
- Respect people's time: read the docs before asking, and follow up politely
- Credit others for their ideas and work

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal attacks or public shaming in issues, reviews or chat
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Spamming issues or pull requests

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the club maintainers privately (add the contact email or chat handle here). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block contributors who break this code of conduct.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/). Maintainers may adopt its full text.
